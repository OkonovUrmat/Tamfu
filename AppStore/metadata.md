### Description (4000)
Throw a message into the world. Someone, somewhere, will find it.

Tamfu is a calm, curious way to meet people — no endless feeds, no follower counts. Write a thought, a question or a simple hello, seal it in a bottle, and Tamfu delivers it to one random person anywhere on Earth. They can answer, start a conversation, or let it drift on to someone else.

SEND A BOTTLE
• Write something personal, ask a question, or share a moment
• Add photos or videos to your message
• Up to 10 bottles a day — so every one means something
• Follow your bottle’s journey and see where it was found

FIND ONE ON YOUR SHORE
• Bottles wash ashore from around the world
• You only see a first initial, age range and country — the rest is a mystery
• Accept to reply, or skip and it drifts on to someone else

REVEAL YOURSELF, ONE STEP AT A TIME
Your profile is split into categories — hobbies, interests, music, movies & books, lifestyle, personality, photos and more. You decide which are public, which stay hidden, and which you’ll reveal in a chat. When someone shares their hobbies, you’re invited to share yours too. Nothing is ever revealed without your tap.

PLAY TOGETHER
Turn small talk into real conversation with games built into every chat:
• Question Cards — love, childhood, travel, philosophy, dreams and more
• Would You Rather
• Two Truths & a Lie
• Random Challenges

EXPLORE THE WORLD
Discover people by country, city, language, interests and hobbies. Swipe right if you’re interested — when it’s mutual, you’re connected. Or send a connection request with a short note.

SAFE BY DESIGN
• Adults only (18+)
• Every bottle is screened for spam, links, phone numbers and abusive language
• Choose how many bottles you receive per day: 5, 20, 50 or 100
• Hide your city, leave discovery, or stop receiving bottles anytime
• Block and report from any profile, bottle or chat
• Delete your account right inside the app

Beautiful in light and dark mode, inspired by the ocean at night.

Connect the real backend (see BACKEND.md) before submitting for review — use TestFlight until then. Once the backend is live, delete this block.

How to test:
1. Launch the app and tap “Explore with a demo profile” (no account or password needed).
2. Bottle tab: tap “Send a message to the world”, write a message (optionally attach a photo/video) and send. Daily limit: 10. Incoming bottles appear on the shore within about a minute — tap the banner or the umbrella icon, then Accept or Skip.
3. Chats tab: open Maria’s conversation. Tap “Reveal” or the sparkles icon to share a profile category (explicit confirmation screen). Tap the dice icon to start a mini-game.
4. Explore tab: swipe cards, tap a card to view the profile, send a connection request.
5. Profile tab → gear icon: privacy settings, incoming message limits, blocked/reported users, account deletion (Account → Delete account).

User-generated content safeguards (Guideline 1.2):
• Users must confirm they are 18+ and agree to the Community Guidelines / Terms of Use before using the app.
• Text is filtered automatically for abusive language, links, phone numbers and spam before delivery.
• Every profile, bottle and chat has Report and Block in the “•••” menu. Blocking immediately removes the user’s content from the reporter’s feed.
• Reports are reviewed within 24 hours; offending content is removed and users are banned.

Account deletion is available in-app: Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account.

The app does not use tracking or advertising.
