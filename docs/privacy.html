Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Tamfu (“Tamfu”, “we”, “us”) is an app for sending messages in a bottle to random people around the world. This policy explains what information we handle when you use the Tamfu iOS app and this website, why, and the choices you have. Tamfu is operated by Urmat Okonov, Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan.

The short version: we only use your information to run Tamfu. We don't sell it, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. You decide what parts of your profile other people see, and you can delete your account at any time from inside the app.

1. Information you give us

InformationWhy we use it
Account details — name or nickname, date of birth, country, optional cityTo create your account, confirm you are 18 or older, and show your country to the people you talk to.
Profile — photos, videos, bio, status, languages, interests, hobbies, music, movies & books, lifestyle, personality and other details you addTo build your profile. Each category is Public, In chats (shared only when you choose to reveal it in a conversation) or Hidden — you control this in Profile → Profile categories.
Messages and bottles — text, photos and videos you send, game answers and revealsTo deliver them to the recipient you're talking to, and to keep your conversation history.
Settings — privacy choices, daily message limits, discovery and notification preferencesTo respect your choices, e.g. never deliver more bottles than your daily limit.
Reports and blocksTo keep the community safe and stop people you've blocked from contacting you.
Support requestsTo answer you when you email us.

2. Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data such as device model, iOS version, app version and crash diagnostics, used to fix bugs and keep the app reliable.
  • Usage counters such as how many bottles you've sent or received today, used to enforce daily limits and prevent spam.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, browsing history, advertising identifier or health data. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs that track you.

3. Photos and videos

Tamfu only accesses the specific photos or videos you choose with the iOS photo picker. We never scan your library. Media you send is screened for safety (for example, to block explicit or violent content) before it is delivered.

4. What other people can see

  • When you send a bottle: the recipient sees your message, any media you attached, your first initial, age range (e.g. “20s”), country, languages and status. Your city is shown only if “Show my city” is on.
  • In Explore: only the categories you've marked Public, if “Appear in global discovery” is on.
  • In a chat: the categories you explicitly reveal by tapping “Reveal”. Nothing is ever revealed automatically.

5. Safety and moderation

To protect users, bottles, messages and media may be checked automatically for spam, links, phone numbers, scams and abusive or explicit content. When you report someone, the reported content and related conversation may be reviewed by a person. We may suspend accounts that break our Terms of Use.

6. How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only:

  • with the people you choose to communicate with, as described above;
  • with service providers that host our servers, store media, send push notifications (Apple Push Notification service) and help with content moderation — they may only use it to provide services to us;
  • if required by law, or to protect the safety of users or the public.

7. Data stored on your device

Your profile and settings are also stored on your iPhone so the app works quickly. Signing out removes your profile from the device.

8. How long we keep information

  • Bottles that nobody answers expire automatically after 72 hours.
  • Your account, profile, messages and media are kept until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account (Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account), we delete your data within 30 days, except where we must keep limited information to comply with the law, resolve disputes or handle safety reports. Backups are overwritten within 90 days.

9. Your rights and choices

  • View and edit your profile at any time in the app.
  • Change who can see each profile category, hide your city, leave discovery, limit incoming bottles (5, 20, 50 or 100 per day) or stop receiving bottles.
  • Block and report other users.
  • Turn notifications off in the app or in iOS Settings.
  • Delete your account directly in the app.
  • Request a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing okonov.okonov@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA), you may have additional rights, including to object to or restrict processing and to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Age requirement

Tamfu is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor is using Tamfu, please report the profile in the app or email us, and we will remove the account.

11. Security

We use industry-standard measures such as encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and restricted access to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't share passwords, financial information or your home address in conversations.

12. International transfers

Tamfu connects people worldwide, so your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Tamfu evolves. We'll change the date at the top and, for important changes, let you know in the app.

14. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: okonov.okonov@gmail.com
