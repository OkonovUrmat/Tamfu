Frequently asked questions

How does a bottle find someone?

When you throw a bottle, Tamfu picks one random adult who is currently accepting bottles, hasn't reached their daily limit and hasn't blocked you. If they skip it, the bottle drifts on to someone else. Unanswered bottles sink after 72 hours.

How many bottles can I send?

Up to 10 per day. The counter resets at midnight. Limits keep every bottle meaningful and the ocean free of spam.

Can I receive fewer messages?

Yes. Go to Profile → Settings → Privacy settings and choose a daily limit of 5, 20, 50 or 100 incoming bottles, or turn receiving off entirely.

What does the other person see about me?

With a bottle: your first initial, age range, country, languages and status. Everything else stays private until you tap “Reveal” in a chat. You can set each profile category to Public, In chats or Hidden in Profile → Profile categories.

How do I block or report someone?

Open their profile, bottle or chat, tap the ••• menu and choose Block or Report. Blocked people can't see you, message you or send you bottles. They aren't notified.

How do I hide my city or leave Explore?

Profile → Settings → Privacy settings: turn off “Show my city” and/or “Appear in global discovery”.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account, then type DELETE to confirm. Your profile, conversations and bottles are deleted permanently.

Why was my message blocked?

To keep conversations safe, bottles can't contain links, phone numbers, contact handles, spam or abusive language. Try rephrasing it in your own words.
