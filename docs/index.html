Tamfu delivers your message in a bottle to one random person, somewhere on Earth. Answer, skip, or start a conversation that slowly reveals who you both are.

Meet people without the pressure

No feeds to scroll, no follower counts. Just one message, one stranger, and a little curiosity.

🍾

Send a bottle

Write a thought, a question or a hello — add a photo or video — and let it drift to one random person. Up to 10 bottles a day keeps every one meaningful.

🌊

Find one on your shore

Open bottles from around the world. You only see a first initial, age range and country. Accept to reply, or skip and it drifts on to someone else.

✨

Reveal yourself slowly

Hobbies, music, favorite films, personality — you choose what to share and when. “Maria revealed her hobbies. Reveal yours too?”

🎲

Play together

Question Cards, Would You Rather, Two Truths & a Lie and small challenges turn small talk into real conversation.

🧭

Explore the world

Discover people by country, language and interests. When interest is mutual, you're connected.

🛡️

You're in control

Set how many messages you receive per day, hide your city, leave discovery, block and report — anytime.

Safe by design

  • Adults only — everyone on Tamfu is 18 or older.
  • Every bottle is screened for spam, links, phone numbers and abusive language before delivery.
  • Personal details are never shared automatically — only when you tap “Reveal”.
  • Daily limits on sending and receiving, so no one is ever overwhelmed.
  • Block and report from any profile, bottle or chat. Reports are reviewed by a person.
